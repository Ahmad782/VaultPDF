VaultPDF is an offline PDF utility application designed to help users view and manage PDF files. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. ### Information We Collect VaultPDF does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal information to our servers. The app may access: * PDF files that you choose to open or process. * Device storage only when required to read or save PDF files. All processing is performed **locally on your device**. ### Internet Connection VaultPDF is designed to work offline. However, if advertisements are enabled, the app may connect to third-party advertising services (such as Google AdMob) to display ads. ### Advertising VaultPDF may display advertisements provided by **Google AdMob**. Google and its partners may collect certain non-personal information, such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address, and app usage data, in accordance with their own privacy policies. Learn more: * https://policies.google.com/privacy * https://support.google.com/admob ### Data Storage All PDF files and any generated documents remain on your device unless you choose to share or export them.
